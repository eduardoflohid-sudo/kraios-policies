Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the Kraios Development Alerts program handles information used to send private software-development SMS notifications to its owner.

Program and purpose

Kraios Development Alerts is a single-recipient, non-marketing SMS notification service operated by its registered proprietor for software-development monitoring. Notifications concern approved development tasks, GitHub pull requests, test results, code reviews, and development errors. The program does not send trading instructions or approve financial transactions.

Information processed

When SMS delivery is enabled, the program processes the owner's designated mobile phone number, SMS delivery and status information, and event details needed to construct notifications (such as a GitHub issue or pull request link, a test result, or a review status). It may retain minimal delivery-deduplication records and message identifiers needed to operate the service and limit repeat alerts.

How information is used

Information is used only to prepare and deliver requested development notifications, troubleshoot delivery, prevent duplicate notifications, and maintain the service. Twilio and telecommunications carriers process information as necessary to send and deliver SMS. We do not use this information for advertising, lead generation, or promotional campaigns.

SMS privacy and sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information and SMS consent data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional activities. Operational service providers may process information strictly to deliver the requested messages.

Consent and your choices

The program is intended solely for its owner. The owner decides whether to enable messaging and configures their own mobile number as the exclusive destination. No public registration or subscription is offered. Once delivery is enabled, the recipient may reply STOP to opt out of SMS, subject to the sender's configured Twilio keyword handling, and may also disable delivery in private configuration. Reply HELP for assistance.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with development events. The system defaults to at most 12 regular SMS notifications per day, plus one possible limit-reached notification. Message and data rates may apply.

Security and retention

Access to the notifier's configuration is limited to its operator. Credentials and destination phone numbers are not published in the source repository. Delivery and deduplication records may be retained for operational purposes; they are not sold or shared for marketing. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the program changes. For questions, visit the public support issue tracker without posting personal details. The companion Terms & Conditions describe the SMS program.
