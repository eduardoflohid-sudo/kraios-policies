Privacy Policy

How LAVQO Bot Alerts handles the information used to send its text messages.

Program and purpose

LAVQO Bot Alerts is an owner-only, non-marketing SMS program run by a sole proprietor. It sends daily results of the LAVQO paper-trading bot (a simulated account with no real money) and urgent bot status alerts. It does not send advertising, investment advice, or trade recommendations.

Information collected

The mobile number entered on the SMS sign-up form, the record of consent, and message delivery status from our SMS provider.

How information is used

Only to send the requested alerts, confirm delivery, honor STOP and HELP requests, and keep the service running. Twilio and the wireless carriers process the number as needed to deliver messages.

SMS privacy and sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never sold, rented, or shared with any third party. Service providers such as Twilio process data only to deliver messages.

Your choices

Reply STOP at any time to opt out; you will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies, about 1–3 messages per weekday and no more than 5 per day. Message and data rates may apply.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the program changes. Questions: use the public support issue tracker, without posting personal details. See also the Terms & Conditions.
