Opt-in process

How people opt in

The only way to join LAVQO Bot Alerts is the web form at sms-signup.html.

Steps

  1. The owner opens the SMS sign-up page.
  2. They enter their mobile number.
  3. They check the consent box, which is unchecked by default. Its wording is shown below.
  4. They press Sign up for text alerts.
  5. A confirmation text is sent with the program name, frequency, rates, and HELP/STOP instructions.

Consent wording shown next to the checkbox

I agree to receive recurring automated text messages from LAVQO Bot Alerts at the mobile number provided, with daily paper-trading bot results and urgent bot status alerts. Message frequency varies, about 1–3 messages per weekday and no more than 5 per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Enrollment is limited to the project owner's own number. There is no purchased list, no keyword marketing, and no third-party numbers.
