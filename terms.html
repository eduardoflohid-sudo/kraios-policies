Terms & Conditions

These terms apply to the single-recipient Kraios Development Alerts SMS notification program.

Program description

Kraios Development Alerts sends informational notifications about software-development activity, including Claude task progress, GitHub pull requests, automated tests, Codex code reviews, and automation interruptions. It is intended exclusively for the operator's own phone number. It is not a marketing program, a trading service, or a means to approve financial transactions or software merges.

SMS terms

Program: Kraios Development Alerts.

Consent: The sole recipient expressly requests SMS notifications and controls the designated destination in private application configuration. No public SMS subscription is offered.

Message frequency: Varies with development activity. The notifier defaults to a maximum of 12 regular messages per day, plus one optional limit-reached notice.

Message and data rates may apply. Charges, if any, depend on the recipient's mobile plan.

STOP: Reply STOP to the sending number to opt out of further messages once delivery is active. The owner may also disable the notifier through private configuration.

HELP: Reply HELP for assistance once the sender's Twilio keyword support is active. Alternatively, use the public support issue tracker; never post personal information there.

Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages.

Approval and safety

SMS messages are informational. No text message or reply constitutes authorization to merge code, deploy software, execute trades, or spend funds. The owner must independently review and approve such actions through authenticated services such as GitHub.

Availability

Delivery depends on GitHub, the notifier service, Twilio, and participating telecommunications carriers. Messages can be delayed, fail, or be unavailable. No uptime or delivery guarantee is made.
