LAVQO Bot Alerts

Text-message status alerts about the LAVQO paper-trading bot, sent only to the project's owner.

LAVQO is a personal software project run by a sole proprietor. Its bot trades a simulated (paper) brokerage account with no real money. LAVQO Bot Alerts texts the owner a short daily summary of the bot's paper results after the market closes, plus an occasional urgent alert when the bot stops, hits its daily loss limit, or needs attention.

Single recipient. The only person enrolled is the owner, who signed up using the SMS sign-up form and checked the consent box. There is no marketing list, and messages never contain advertising, investment advice, or recommendations to buy or sell anything.

Messaging details

Message frequency varies: about 1–3 messages per weekday, no more than 5 per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.
